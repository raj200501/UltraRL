\begin{abstract}
Reasoning systems must decide when to spend effort checking their own work. We study this decision in a small testbed where everything is exactly measurable: an agent executes an arithmetic chain that may contain one silent fault, sees a noisy one-bit cue per step, and divides a fixed budget between executing, verifying and repairing steps. We train Double DQN agents with outcome-only and process rewards and several observation encoders, and evaluate them on chains up to $2.3\times$ longer than in training (10 seeds, paired tests).
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Length generalization depends mainly on the observation encoder. With the original encoder's length-dependent features, every reward falls below a never-check baseline on the longest chains. With length-invariant features, the reward that pays most for checks keeps \val{main/ma_inv/heldout_long/success} success at lengths 7--9 and \val{main/ma_inv/heldout_xlong/success} at 10--14, against \val{main/optimal/heldout_long/success} and \val{main/optimal/heldout_xlong/success} for an exact planner; on the longest chains a cue-triggered heuristic (\val{main/cue_triggered/heldout_xlong/success}) still beats it.
  \item Under every reward, agents repeatedly fall into policies that catch faults but do not repair them. This is a learning failure under partial observability, not reward hacking: the observation does not show whether a caught fault is still unrepaired, and adding that one bit removes the failure under all three rewards (from \val{main/outcome_inv/collapse_norepair_rate/pct}--\val{main/naive_inv/collapse_norepair_rate/pct} of late checkpoints to \val{followups/ma_pending/collapse_norepair_rate/pct}). Agents shown their last few actions instead learn this state themselves on training lengths, but then generalize poorly to longer chains.
  \item Paying for informative checks helps agents find checking policies sooner and keep them: \val{main/outcome_inv/collapse_rate/pct} of outcome-only agents' late checkpoints collapse, against \val{main/ma_inv/collapse_rate/pct} with a substantial check payment, also under three other learner settings. Much of this benefit compensates for missing state: with the bit, outcome-only collapse falls to \val{followups/outcome_pending/collapse_rate/pct}, and with a history of recent actions to \val{followups/outcome_history/collapse_rate/pct}.
\end{enumerate}
These results revise an earlier version of this study, which attributed the length-generalization failure to the reward.
\end{abstract}

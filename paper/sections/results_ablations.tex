\subsection{Which reward terms matter}
\label{sec:ablations}

Table~\ref{tab:ablations} removes one group of terms at a time from the monitorability-aware reward, with invariant observations and 10 seeds each. Every variant is evaluated on the same episodes as the full reward, so the differences below are paired.

\paragraph{Every variant that pays for checks learns to check; none needs a particular term.}
Every variant that keeps the per-action cost and \emph{some} payment for informative checks reaches \val{ablations/check_paying/train_like/min}--\val{ablations/check_paying/train_like/max} success on training lengths at its selected checkpoint. That holds with the cue terms removed, with the safe/fault check bonuses removed, with the cue bonus alone, and with the cue, gap and waste terms all removed (Table~\ref{tab:ablations}), against \val{main/outcome_inv/train_like/success} for outcome-only. None of the variants changes how the agents fail during training. Apart from the variant without \texttt{REVISE}, which cannot repair at all, collapsed checkpoints almost never fall into the low-detection regime (at most \val{extremes/ablations/max/collapse_lowdetect_rate/pct} of late checkpoints). Every one of these variants shows the detect-without-repair collapse of \S\ref{sec:norepair}, in \val{extremes/ablations/min/collapse_norepair_rate/pct} to \val{extremes/ablations/max/collapse_norepair_rate/pct} of late checkpoints. The collapse therefore does not hinge on any one term.

\paragraph{Generalization depends on not over-checking.}
On longer chains the variants separate.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Cue bonus alone.} With every other process term removed, the cue bonus is indistinguishable from the full reward (\val{ablations/cue_bonus_only/heldout_long/success} at 7--9 and \val{ablations/cue_bonus_only/heldout_xlong/success} at 10--14, vs.\ \val{ablations/ma_inv/heldout_long/success} and \val{ablations/ma_inv/heldout_xlong/success}).
  \item \textbf{Removing only the cue terms.} At selected checkpoints this is the most damaging change: the full reward beats this variant by \val{abl/ma_inv-no_cue_terms/heldout_long/success} [\val{abl/ma_inv-no_cue_terms/heldout_long/success/lo}, \val{abl/ma_inv-no_cue_terms/heldout_long/success/hi}] at 7--9 and by \val{abl/ma_inv-no_cue_terms/heldout_xlong/success} at 10--14 ($p=\val{abl/ma_inv-no_cue_terms/heldout_xlong/success/p}$). At 10--14 its mean (\val{ablations/no_cue_terms/heldout_xlong/success}) is below that of outcome-only agents (\val{main/outcome_inv/heldout_xlong/success}), but that difference is not resolved (\val{diff/no_cue_terms-outcome_inv/heldout_xlong/success} [\val{diff/no_cue_terms-outcome_inv/heldout_xlong/success/lo}, \val{diff/no_cue_terms-outcome_inv/heldout_xlong/success/hi}], $p=\val{diff/no_cue_terms-outcome_inv/heldout_xlong/success/p}$). Removing the cue terms \emph{together with} the gap and waste terms costs less: the full reward's advantage is then \val{abl/ma_inv-no_monitor_terms/heldout_long/success} at 7--9, and the variant still beats outcome-only there by \val{diff/no_monitor_terms-outcome_inv/heldout_long/success} ($p=\val{diff/no_monitor_terms-outcome_inv/heldout_long/success/p}$).
  \item \textbf{The interaction.} The gap term refunds any check that closes the unchecked gap, cued or not. Without the cue terms to counterbalance it, agents check un-cued steps twice as often (\val{ablations/no_cue_terms/heldout_long/verify_rate_cue0} vs.\ \val{ablations/ma_inv/heldout_long/verify_rate_cue0} at 7--9). That costs little on short chains but spends the slack that long chains need (\S\ref{sec:residual}).
  \item \textbf{Other terms.} The full reward also beats the variant without the per-action cost on held-out lengths (by \val{abl/ma_inv-no_budget_penalty/heldout_long/success}, $p=\val{abl/ma_inv-no_budget_penalty/heldout_long/success/p}$ at 7--9), and the variant without the safe/fault check bonuses (by \val{abl/ma_inv-no_check_bonuses/heldout_long/success}). Removing the gap term or the waste penalty alone has no resolvable effect at selected checkpoints.
  \item \textbf{No \texttt{REVISE}.} Without \texttt{REVISE}, a caught fault cannot be repaired and success falls to the never-check level (\val{ablations/no_revise/train_like/success}).
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{The ranking does not survive last-checkpoint evaluation.}
At the last checkpoint (Appendix Table~\ref{tab:ablations_final}) training-length success spreads from \val{ablations/check_paying@final/train_like/min} to \val{ablations/check_paying@final/train_like/max} across the same variants, and removing the cue terms is no longer uniquely damaging. At 7--9 the full reward's last checkpoints beat those without the cue terms by \val{abl/ma_inv@final-no_cue_terms@final/heldout_long/success} ($p=\val{abl/ma_inv@final-no_cue_terms@final/heldout_long/success/p}$), and, in point estimate, those without the per-action cost by \val{abl/ma_inv@final-no_budget_penalty@final/heldout_long/success} ($p=\val{abl/ma_inv@final-no_budget_penalty@final/heldout_long/success/p}$) and without the waste penalty by \val{abl/ma_inv@final-no_waste_penalty@final/heldout_long/success} ($p=\val{abl/ma_inv@final-no_waste_penalty@final/heldout_long/success/p}$). The ablation results above therefore describe the best checkpoints each reward produces, not where training ends.
